The audience

You are a god. Numan still decides.

Gods suggest. Numan listens, weighs the request against how he is feeling, and then honors it, haggles, puts it off, or refuses with a lot of attitude.

What a god can ask for

🏗️ Build

Campfire, straw bed, workbench or hut.

🔨 Craft

Rope, stone axe, stone pickaxe or berry stew.

🪵 Gather

Wood, stone, fiber or oil berries.

💃 Activity

Stargaze, sit by the fire, watch clouds, dance, or wave at the sky.

🛌 Basics

Eat, sleep or explore.

What happens to a request

  1. It joins the queue. Up to 5 requests wait at once. Extras are dropped.
  2. Numan thinks. The AI brain sees the request, his needs, and his mood.
  3. He answers in character. He honors it, delays it, or refuses, with a short line.
  4. Survival comes first. If he is starving or exhausted, he takes care of that before anything else.

Example refusal: "A volcano inside the town? Bold. No." Refusals are part of the fun, and they stop chaos from taking over the island.

Today vs. later

For the curious: what a request looks like

Requests are small JSON messages. The sim checks each one and ignores anything it does not understand.

{ "type": "goal", "goal": { "type": "build", "kind": "hut" } }

Safety: viewer text is cleaned before use: control characters, hidden tricks and extra-long messages are removed. Text only ever shows as plain words, never as code.
