Where we are

What is done, what is being hardened, what is next.

Versions are in CHANGELOG.md. Nothing here is a promise with a date on it.

Done

0.4.0

Memory and a nightly journal

Numan remembers notable moments and searches them by meaning. Each night he writes a journal entry. Memory carries over when a new era starts.

0.3.0

The AI brain

A local model (Ornith 9B) picks his next goal and says a short line about every 15 seconds. The sim checks every answer before using it.

0.2.0

The island and the sim

A life sim with a clock, 8 needs, resources that regrow, recipes, buildings, a planner, and god requests. The screen draws what the sim says.

In progress: hardening

Shipped after 0.4.0 but not yet tagged. This is the security and stability pass.

  • Host and Origin checks, rate limits, client cap, and an operator token for reset and speed.
  • Text from viewers is cleaned before anything uses it.
  • The game clock now runs on real elapsed time. It had been about 9% slow on Windows.
  • A crash that a single odd message could cause is fixed.
  • 89 automated tests, plus a 27-check live-server test.

Next, roughly in order

1. Stability pack

Rotating backups and save migrations. A stuck-loop watchdog, a health page, and a crash-restart test for start.bat.

2. Efficiency pack

Send only changes to the screen, draw repeated objects in one batch, and cap how long each brain answer can run.

3. Voice

Pick a text-to-speech option after measuring it on the streaming PC. See AI models for the three candidates.

Later

More island tiers

Kiln and bricks, paths, a villager who helps, and later a forge, farming, and weather.

Chat hookup

A Twitch bridge would turn chat into god requests. It needs a word filter and rate limits first.

Awareness arc

Numan slowly notices he is being watched. Each era would end with a finale.
