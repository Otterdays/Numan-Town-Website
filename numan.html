Meet the robot

Numan

A small, round-headed robot with a glowing visor and a pink light on his antenna. He is vain, lazy, sarcastic and sure he is the greatest builder in the sky. Under all that chrome, he cares about his island more than anything.

His vibe

  • Likes: sunsets, tidy rows of crops, being thanked
  • Dislikes: rain on his joints, mess, being rushed
  • Says: "Good morning, gods!" · "One more brick." · "Noted, divine ones."

His 8 needs

Like a Tamagotchi: every need drains over time. Let one get too low and he slows down or gets grumpy. Survival always wins, so he will not skip dinner because chat wanted a tower.

⚡ ChargePower. Sleep refills it.
🛢️ FuelHis food. Oil berries and stew.
🔧 ConditionRepairs and wear.
😴 RestSleep and sitting down.
🎉 FunDancing, stargazing, hobbies.
💬 SocialCompany. Chat counts too, later.
🧠 CuriosityExploring and learning.
🏠 ComfortHow nice his home feels.

His life plan

With nothing else to do, Numan follows this plan. It is the stage-by-stage path from bare grass to a home.

  1. Campfire. His first building.
  2. Straw bed. Where he sleeps, so his charge refills.
  3. Stone axe. A tool for gathering wood.
  4. Stone pickaxe. A tool for gathering stone.
  5. Workbench. Where he crafts.
  6. Hut. His first real home.

Right now the plan finishes by day 2, then he has spare time. More tech levels are planned.

Two brains, one Numan

🧭 Instincts

Simple rules that always run. "Charge is low? Go to bed." This keeps him alive even if everything else breaks.

🧠 AI brain

A local language model. About every 15 seconds it picks his next goal and says a short thought out loud. If it is off, he just follows instincts.

Planned, not built: a story arc called Awareness. Numan slowly notices that someone is watching, from "curious" to "knowing." Each era would end with a finale.
